# 十项高级修辞：模板与案例

命中原则：**每项至少一处，尽量分散在不同段落**。批改网不检测这一项，但它是用户的核心评分需求。

## 一、用名词替代动词

英语惯用「名词短语」而非「动词 + 副词」来表达身份、能力与判断。

| 中文 | 弱（动词） | 强（名词） |
|---|---|---|
| 他游泳快 | He swims fast. | He is a fast swimmer. |
| 她做饭糟 | She cooks terribly. | She is a terrible cook. |
| 我宣传绿色理念 | I volunteer to promote green ideas. | I am a communicator of green ideas. |

应试常用配式：

- 身份三连：`I am a learner of X who …, a volunteer who …, and a communicator who …`
- 判断名词化：`X deserves rigorous analysis` / `X merits equal attention` / `X is worth a close look`
- 者型名词：`Every photovoltaic engineer in Chengdu … is, in essence, a speaker of the same tongue.`

## 二、用连续介词代替多个动词

一个动词 + 一串介词，省掉三四个动词。

- `The cat jumped from the roof across the yard into the bushes.`
- `The view from the top of the mountain at sunset is breathtaking.`

作文高频：

- `from the design of smarter energy systems to the art of telling their stories`
- `from anti-slip flooring to intelligent monitoring devices`

写作要点：介词后跟名词短语，形成 `from A of B to C of D` 的双层结构，最有层次感。

## 三、比较级表达最高级（否定词 + 比较级）

- `Nothing is more precious than health.`
- `I have never read a more moving story.`

应试**标配两处**：

- 首段立论：`Nothing is more defining of our generation's mission than the <topic> boom …`
  / `Nothing is more revealing of a society's conscience than the way it cares for its elderly …`
- 尾段升华：`no mission is more worthy of youth than the harmony between humanity and nature`

## 四、not … enough（不足以到某种程度）

- `He is not old enough to go to school.`

作文高频：

`Yet enthusiasm alone is not enough, because it is not rigorous enough to replace knowledge, nor strong enough to move policy.`

`X alone is not enough` + 两个 `not adj enough to` 连用，是最稳的高分句群。

## 五、not so much A as B（与其说……不如说……，强调 B）

- `Success depends not so much on intelligence as on persistence.`

作文高频：

- `Green technology travels not so much through markets as through meaning.`
- `The movement thrives not so much on policy documents as on concrete human connections.`

放在**机理段首句之后的第一句**，判断味最足。

## 六、can't / never … too …（越……越好）

- `You can't be too careful when driving at night.`
- `I never have too much respect for my tutor.`

作文高频：

- `One can't be too careful when we care for our only planet.`
- `One can't be too patient when we accompany the elderly.`

## 七、部分否定

- `All that glitters is not gold.`（不都是，而非都不是）
- `Not every student passed the exam.`

作文高频：`Not every young person is a scientist / a professional caregiver …`

## 八、双重否定

- `There is no one who doesn't know him in this town.`
- `Nothing is impossible.`

作文高频：`there is hardly anyone in this generation who doesn't feel the call of the era`

与部分否定**连成一句**效果最好：

`Not every young person is a scientist, yet there is hardly anyone in this generation who doesn't feel the call of the era.`

## 九、否定前置（主句否定，从句肯定）

- `I don't think he is qualified for the job.`（不说 I think he is not …）
- `She doesn't suppose they will show up on time.`

作文高频：`As a young person of the new era, I don't consider passive admiration a sufficient answer.`

补充：把 `I don't think` 换成 `I don't consider`，可同时完成词汇升级（避免 think 这个高频基础词）。

## 十、定语后置

汉语定语一律前置；英语中**词组与从句作定语一律后置**。

- `The construction of the new bridge across the river was completed last month.`
- `It is a ship, already visible from the shore, whose masthead can be seen looming over the horizon; it is the morning sun, rising and shining in the east from the mountaintop; it is a baby, stirring in its mother's womb, soon to come into the world.`

应试高频后置形式：

- 介词短语：`the illumination of remote African villages through solar micro-grids`
- 形容词短语：`people aged 60 and above`
- 从句：`professionals who devote themselves to green technology`、`a future in which humanity and nature coexist in harmony`
